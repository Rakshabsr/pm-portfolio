Product Approach

How I think about building products.

01

Start from the operational pain

I sit with the teams doing the work — what takes two days, what breaks under load, what nobody has bandwidth to fix. That's where the PRD starts.

02

Build the prototype, not just the spec

When I can write the code, I do. When I can't, I go deep enough with engineering that the trade-offs are mine — not assumptions handed across a wall.

03

Measure what the team stopped doing

The metric isn't features shipped. It's hours reclaimed, errors eliminated, manual processes that no longer need a person clicking through them.

04

Ship first, earn scope next

The BoardDocs tool started as a personal automation. Leadership saw the results and funded the initiative. Scope follows outcomes, not slide decks.
